# Social links at the bottom of the website

The academic and social buttons are in the website footer, after the contact form and animated statistical distributions. They appear just before the copyright row.

To set your personal profiles, open `assets/site.js` and find `OPTIONAL_SOCIAL`. Replace the blank `profileUrl` values with your own actual HTTPS account URLs. Demo URLs go to the platforms’ official main pages and are **not** personal profiles.

Upload `index.html` and `assets/style.css` together to apply this layout change. Other files are supplied for completeness.
