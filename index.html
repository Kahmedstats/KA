About me

I work on statistical methodology and applied research, with interests in causal inference, missing data and biostatistics. My work connects rigorous quantitative analysis to questions in public health and other data-rich fields.

Research directions

Causal inference and discovery

Estimating causal effects, observational analysis, and high-dimensional data.

Missing data and imputation

Reliable estimation with incomplete observations and covariate adjustment.

Biostatistics and applied analysis

Statistical modeling for public health, survey research, and evaluation.
