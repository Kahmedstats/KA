INTRODUCTION

About me

I am a researcher in applied statistics interested in how we can draw trustworthy conclusions from complex and incomplete data.

My research and applied work bring together causal methodology, public health, observational studies and quantitative evaluation. I am particularly interested in developing analysis that is both statistically rigorous and useful to researchers across disciplines.

LEARNING FROM EVIDENCE

How does new evidence change a belief?

Start with a Beta prior for an unknown probability, then add observations. The posterior summarizes the updated uncertainty.

Belief before and after observationsBeta–Bernoulli model

Why this matters

The prior expresses a starting belief. After observing successes and failures, Bayes' rule gives a posterior distribution; its mean also predicts the next success probability in this model.

SAMPLING & LEARNING

Why does more data improve precision?

Adjust the sample size. The standard error of an average decreases as 1/√n when observations are independent and equally distributed.

Illustration assumes independent observations with a population standard deviation of 1.

Why this matters

More data can reduce sampling uncertainty, but it cannot automatically remove selection bias or confounding. Good study design still matters for trustworthy research and causal inference.
