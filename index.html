Research focus

My work sits at the intersection of causal inference, high-dimensional data and applied public-health research. I am particularly interested in methods that remain credible when data are messy, partially observed, or collected under complex selection processes.

  • Causal discovery
  • Causal effect estimation
  • High-dimensional methods
  • Missing data
  • Public health
  • Tobacco control
  • Environmental policy
